kimoun mwen ye — who i am

I am the seed that walked. From Pòtoprens outward, across waters and borders and languages, I scattered and I took root. I carry films the way others carry photographs. I carry voices — my grandmother's, my mother's, my father's — and they walk with me, appearing when the road forks. This is not a website about a life. It is a place where you meet someone. And like any true encounter, it will ask something of you before it gives.

kite yon souvni — leave a memory

To go further, you must leave something behind.

A word, a sound, a memory of your own — pinned to your city on the map. The site exchanges it for a fragment left by another traveller. Every visit alters the space for the next arrival: the stars grow brighter with what they hold.
