The Threshold — three intensities

Phase 1 · design language exploration · Ésery Mondésir

Variation A

La Retenue — restraint

A cinematic triptych. Three tall doors share the screen; hovering one slowly opens it wider and tints the room. The calmest of the three — fragmentation is implied, not performed.

leaning: Salomon Ligthelm

 Variation B

La Dérive — drift

The doors float untethered over a breathing color field. Hovering a door tints the entire atmosphere toward its mood. The name sits behind everything like a watermark of the self.

leaning: Má Sài Gòn

 Variation C

La Rupture — rupture

Torn scraps at wrong angles, a title that twitches, doors that shy away from the cursor before letting you in. The most unstable — the site itself misbehaves, deliberately.

leaning: Dada Data

All three share the same bones: three doors (Watch / Listen / Read), the Dérive escape hatch, the EN·FR·Kreyòl switcher, grain, and the choublak red. They differ only in how much instability the surface admits. The final Threshold can also blend two — e.g. B's mood tinting inside A's restraint.
