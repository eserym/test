Three display faces, and the reading voice they still need.

Gemini is right: Velvetyne-family display faces are the correct register — raw, textured, fragmented, made for research-creation, not for legibility. But all three are display faces. Grann's counsel is a paragraph a stranger must actually read; the chamber echoes are full sentences. So the real decision isn't one font — it's a system: one experimental voice for the big moments, one honest voice for reading, one typewriter for the archive labels. Below: each face on your own words, then the pairing question.

What I'd build

Solide Mirage as the display voice everywhere it counts — the proverb, chamber titles, place names when they announce. Pilowlava for exactly one thing: the name at the portal, a molten logotype. Climate Crisis not as a font you read but as a mechanic — the erosion axis driving RESPÈ into being and the veil tearing, on threshold moments only (needs the variable file dropped in).

For the reading voice I lean Option B — the counsel is the emotional core of the door; monospace would freeze it. Warm serif for Grann, typewriter kept strictly for the archive labels (km, place-kind, stamps). That's the tension the whole work lives in: rupture and tenderness, held in two type voices.

display → Solide Mirage (proverb, chamber titles, announcements)
logotype → Pilowlava (the name, portal only)
mechanic → Climate Crisis (erosion on RESPÈ + veil; variable file)
reading → Spectral / Newsreader (Grann's counsel, echoes, longer text)
label → Courier Prime (km, place-kind, stamps, the archive voice)
