palèt lwa · grenn pwomennen · a cosmology of color

Each kind of place glows with the lwa who governs it.

Not arbitrary — a mapping. Legba stands at the gate, because he is the one who opens the way. The communities scattered across the water glow with the sea-spirits who carried them. The work burns with Ogou's iron and fire. The land glows with Gran Bwa's green. The ancestors, with Gede's violet. The color of a star tells you what kind of crossing it holds.

Papa Legba
the portal · the threshold · the crossroads

Guardian of the gate, master of the crossroads, the first lwa saluted in any ceremony — “Papa Legba, ouvri baryè pou mwen.” Open the gate for me. The whole site opens on his rite: Onè asked, Respè granted, the way unbarred. His colors are the straw-gold of the crossroads at dusk, bone white, dried-blood red, the dark earth of the path.

Danbala Wèdo
the core · the origin · Pòtoprens

The white serpent, oldest of the lwa, source and purity — the still center around which everything coils. At the heart of the sky sits Pòtoprens, from which every distance is measured. It burns Danbala's white: the origin, luminous and undivided.

Gran Bwa
the wellsprings · the land · sending regions

Lwa of the deep forest, of leaves and roots and healing herbs — the wild green from which everything grows. Ayiti's own inner constellation, the towns and regions that send their children out, glow with his green. The seed comes from the tree; the tree stands in Gran Bwa's woods.

Ogou Feray
the work · the films · what was forged

Warrior lwa of iron, fire, and force — the blacksmith, the one who cuts and forges. The films and the exhibitions, the work made and shown, burn with Ogou's red: creation as heat and labor, the cane fields, the flicker of the projector's gate.

→ travay stars — Tijuana, Havana, the works

Agwe & Lasirèn
the communities · across the water

Sovereign of the sea and the mermaid of its depths — the waters every migrant crossed. The diaspora communities, scattered by boat and plane across the ocean, glow with Agwe's blue-green. The sea that took them is the sea that holds them; their color is the water between.

→ kominote stars — Flatbush, Nassau, Montréal, Paris…
#34518f royal blue · #b23124 her red
Ezili Dantò
both at once · community + work

The fierce mother, protector, hard worker — she carries both blue and red, both tenderness and the scar. The places that are both a community and a site of the work (Miami, Montréal, Toronto) take Dantò's deep blue: the crossing and the labor held in one body.

→ toude stars — Miami, Montréal, Toronto

Gede · Bawon
the ancestors · the guides · the dead who walk

Lwa of death and resurrection, of the ancestors who are never truly gone — irreverent, funny, wise. Grann, Manman, Papa: the guides who appear at the thresholds, who walk beside the seed. They glow with Gede's violet, the color of the ancestral dead who counsel the living.

→ zanset stars — Grann, Manman, Papa

Colors vary by house, by rite (Rada / Petwo), by region. These are the widely-held associations — a starting cosmology, not a fixed doctrine. Tell me where your lineage sees it differently, and the sky changes with you.
